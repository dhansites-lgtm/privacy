Booking Price Rules: Privacy Policy

DhanSites · Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains how DhanSites ("we", "us") handles data in the Booking Price Rules app ("the App") for Wix sites. The App lets a business that takes online bookings charge different prices depending on the day, time or date of an appointment.

1. Who is responsible

The business that installs the App ("the Customer") sets its own price rules and decides what to charge. We provide the software and act as a processor.

2. What the App stores

Only the Customer's price rules: a rule name, which services it covers, days, times, dates, and the price change. The App does not store any client details.

3. What the App reads

  • The Customer's list of services and their standard prices, to show them in the dashboard and to work out the new price.
  • At checkout: the service, date, time, time zone and number of participants of the booking being paid for. This is used only to calculate the price and is not saved.

4. Where data is stored

Price rules are stored in the Customer's own Wix site database (Wix CMS), hosted by Wix. We do not copy anything to our own servers. Wix's handling of stored data is covered by Wix's privacy policy.

5. How data is used

Data is used only to show and edit price rules, to calculate booking prices, and to check the Customer's plan (free or paid). We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

6. Sharing

Data is shared only with the Customer and their site collaborators, with Wix as the hosting platform, and with authorities where the law requires it.

7. Retention and deletion

Rules stay until the Customer deletes them or the site. If the App is uninstalled, the rules may remain in the site's CMS until the Customer deletes them.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Contact the business, or us below.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the "Last updated" date when we do.
