Client Content Checklist: Privacy Policy

DhanSites · Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains how DhanSites ("we", "us") handles data in the Client Content Checklist app ("the App") for Wix sites. The App lets a site owner and the people who build their website share a checklist of content (text, links and files) inside the site's dashboard.

1. Who is responsible

The site owner ("the Customer") controls the content added to their checklist. We provide the software and act as a processor. We do not decide what is collected or how it is used.

2. What the App stores

  • Checklist items: titles, instructions, sections and status.
  • Answers typed into the checklist, links, and feedback notes.
  • Links to files uploaded through the site's own Media Manager. The files stay in the site's Wix media library.

The App is designed for website content such as logos, About text and photos. Please do not use it for passwords, payment details or health information.

From Wix we receive the site's installation identifiers and plan (free or paid) to apply the free plan limit.

3. Where data is stored

All checklist data is stored in the Customer's own Wix site database (Wix CMS), hosted by Wix. We do not copy it to our own servers. Only the site's owner and collaborators with dashboard access can see it. Wix's handling of stored data is covered by Wix's privacy policy.

4. How data is used

Data is used only to show the checklist, its progress and its review status in the dashboard, and to count items for the free plan limit. We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising or use it to train AI models.

5. Sharing

Data is shared only with the Customer and their site collaborators, with Wix as the hosting platform, and with authorities where the law requires it.

6. Retention and deletion

Items stay in the site until the Customer removes them or deletes the site. If the App is uninstalled, the collection may remain in the site's CMS until the Customer deletes it.

7. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and stored encrypted by Wix. Only signed-in dashboard users can read or change checklist items.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Contact the site owner, or us below.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the "Last updated" date when we do.
