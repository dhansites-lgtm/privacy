No-Show Guard: Privacy Policy

DhanSites · Last updated 27 November 2026

This policy explains how DhanSites ("we", "us") handles data in the No-Show Guard app ("the App") for Wix sites. The App helps a business that uses Wix Bookings see which clients miss their appointments, stop chosen clients from booking online, and keep a waitlist that offers cancelled times to clients who asked for them.

1. Who is responsible

The business that installs the App ("the Customer") decides which clients to block and why. We provide the software and act as a processor. Clients with questions about their data should contact the business directly.

2. What the App reads

  • From Wix Bookings: past bookings in the period the Customer chooses, each client's name and email, the booking date, and whether the business marked the booking as No-Show.
  • At checkout: the email address of the person booking, used only to check it against the Customer's block list.
  • When a booking is cancelled or confirmed: the service, time and client email of that booking, used only to offer the freed time to the waitlist and to mark waitlist clients who have booked.

3. What the App stores

  • The block list: email, name, Wix contact ID, whether the client is blocked, the date they were blocked, and an optional private note.
  • The waitlist: for each client who joins from the "Join the Waitlist" form on the Customer's site, their name, email, optional phone number, the service, the days and time of day that suit them, the date they joined, and whether a time was offered or booked.
  • The Customer's settings: the no-show limit, the look-back period, whether automatic blocking is on, and the message shown to blocked clients.

To email a waitlist client, the App finds or creates a contact for them in the Customer's Wix Contacts. The email itself is sent by the Customer's own Wix Automations, which the Customer can edit or switch off.

No-show counts are worked out from Wix Bookings each time the dashboard opens. We do not keep a separate copy of booking history.

4. Where data is stored

Everything above is stored in the Customer's own Wix site database (Wix CMS), hosted by Wix. We do not copy it to our own servers. Only the site owner and collaborators with dashboard access can see it. Wix's handling of stored data is covered by Wix's privacy policy.

5. How data is used

Data is used only to show the no-show list, to block or allow online bookings as the Customer chooses, to email waitlist clients when a matching time opens up, and to check the Customer's plan (free or paid). We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

6. Fairness to clients

A blocked client can still contact the business directly. The App never cancels existing bookings and never charges clients. If the App cannot complete its check, the booking goes ahead.

7. Sharing

Data is shared only with the Customer and their site collaborators, with Wix as the hosting platform, and with authorities where the law requires it.

8. Retention and deletion

Waitlist entries stay until the Customer removes or deletes them. Block list entries stay until the Customer unblocks or removes them, or deletes the site. If the App is uninstalled, the stored data may remain in the site's CMS until the Customer deletes it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Clients: contact the business. Businesses: contact us below.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the "Last updated" date when we do.
