Clinic Intake and Consent: Privacy Policy

DhanSites · Last updated 27 November 2026

This policy explains how DhanSites ("we", "us") handles data in the Clinic Intake and Consent app ("the App") for Wix sites. The App lets a clinic ("the Clinic", our customer) collect patient intake forms and signed consent on its own website.

1. Who is responsible for patient data

  • The Clinic is the controller of the information its patients submit. The Clinic decides what to ask, why, and how long to keep it.
  • We are a processor and service provider to the Clinic. We provide the software and do not decide how patient data is used.
  • Patients with questions about their own data should contact the Clinic directly.

2. What the App collects

From patients, through the Clinic's form:

  • Name, date of birth, email, phone number, emergency contact
  • Reason for visit, area of concern, pain level
  • Medical history, current medications, allergies
  • Consent confirmation, typed signature, date and time of signing, and a copy of the consent text agreed to

This can include health information, which is a special category of personal data in many countries.

From the Clinic's Wix Bookings, when a booking is confirmed or cancelled: the booking ID, the patient's contact ID, name and email, the service name and the appointment time. This is used only to decide whether to send the patient a link to the intake form (patients who already have a form on file are skipped) and is not stored by the App, apart from a short note of the latest result (for example "form email started for Asha Rao") that the Clinic sees on its settings page.

From the Clinic: Wix site and app installation identifiers, the plan (free or paid), and the form settings the Clinic saves. Wix provides installation and billing details to us under its own terms.

3. Where patient data is stored

Submissions and settings are stored in the Clinic's own Wix site database (Wix CMS), hosted by Wix. The App's code runs on Wix's infrastructure.

  • We do not copy patient submissions to our own servers or databases.
  • Only the Clinic's site owners and admin collaborators can read submissions in the Wix dashboard. Site visitors cannot read other people's submissions.
  • Wix's handling of stored data is covered by Wix's privacy policy.

4. How data is used

Patient data is used only to deliver the submitted form to the Clinic, show it in the Clinic's dashboard, notify the Clinic that a new form arrived (patient name only), and count forms each month to apply the free plan limit.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. We access submission content only when the Clinic asks us to for support, and only as needed to resolve the issue.

5. Sharing

Patient data is shared only with the Clinic, with Wix as the hosting platform, and with authorities where the law requires it.

The App can pass a patient's name, email, contact ID, service name, appointment time and the Clinic's form link to Wix Automations in the Clinic's own site, so that automations the Clinic turns on can email the patient (for example a link to fill in the form) or alert the Clinic about a new form. These emails are sent by Wix from the Clinic's site under the Clinic's email settings. Health details from submitted forms are never included.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Submissions stay in the Clinic's site until the Clinic deletes them or deletes its site.
  • If the Clinic uninstalls the App, the data may remain in its Wix CMS until the Clinic deletes it.
  • Patients who want their data deleted should contact the Clinic.

7. Security

  • Data is sent over HTTPS and stored encrypted by Wix.
  • Submissions are readable only by site admins; the public form can only add new submissions.
  • The form checks and limits the size of every field before storing it.

No system is perfectly secure. The Clinic is responsible for who it gives admin access to its Wix site.

8. Health-data regulations

The App is a general intake tool. It is not certified as HIPAA-compliant, and we do not sign Business Associate Agreements. Clinics subject to HIPAA (USA), GDPR special-category rules (EU/UK), PIPEDA or PHIPA (Canada), the DPDP Act (India) or similar laws must decide whether the App and Wix meet their obligations before collecting health information with it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal data. Patients: contact the Clinic. Clinics: contact us below.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children. A parent or guardian should complete forms for patients under the age of consent.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for significant changes, notify Clinics through the App or by email.
