GST Invoices for Bookings: Privacy Policy

DhanSites · Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains how DhanSites ("we", "us") handles data in the GST Invoices for Bookings app ("the App") for Wix sites. The App lets a business create GST tax invoices for its booking orders.

1. Who is responsible

The business that installs the App ("the Customer") is responsible for its invoices and tax details. We provide the software and act as a processor. Clients with questions about their invoices should contact the business.

2. What the App reads

From the Customer's Wix orders that include bookings: order number and date, the client's name, email and billing address, the services booked, quantities, amounts and payment status. This is read to show the list and build invoices, and is not copied elsewhere.

3. What the App stores

  • The Customer's GST details: legal name, GSTIN, address, state, SAC code, GST rate, invoice prefix and footer note.
  • For each invoice created: the order ID and number, the invoice number and the invoice date.

Invoices are generated in the Customer's browser when printed. We do not keep copies of invoices.

4. Where data is stored

Stored data lives in the Customer's own Wix site database (Wix CMS), hosted by Wix. We do not copy anything to our own servers. Wix's handling of stored data is covered by Wix's privacy policy.

5. How data is used

Data is used only to list booking orders, number and print invoices, and check the Customer's plan (free or paid). We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

6. Tax advice

The App calculates CGST, SGST and IGST from the settings the Customer enters. It does not give tax advice. The Customer should confirm rates and SAC codes with their accountant.

7. Sharing

Data is shared only with the Customer and their site collaborators, with Wix as the hosting platform, and with authorities where the law requires it.

8. Retention and deletion

Settings and invoice numbers stay until the Customer deletes them or the site. If the App is uninstalled, they may remain in the site's CMS until the Customer deletes them.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Contact the business, or us below.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the "Last updated" date when we do.
