Privacy policies

DhanSites · Last updated 18 November 2026

DhanSites makes apps for Wix sites. Each app has its own privacy policy:

  • Clinic Intake and Consent: patient intake forms and signed consent for clinics.
  • Client Content Checklist: a shared checklist for collecting website content from clients.
  • No-Show Guard: tracks missed appointments and stops repeat no-shows from booking online.
  • Booking Price Rules: charges different prices for bookings by day, time or date.
  • GST Invoices for Bookings: prints GST tax invoices for bookings.
